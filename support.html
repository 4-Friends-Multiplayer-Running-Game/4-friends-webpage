Support

Answers to the questions people ask most, and a form to report a bug or ask for a feature. You can also write to mateoas@icloud.com.

Questions

What is 4 Friends?

A multiplayer running game for you and three friends: four players racing in the same match. It is in development now.

When can I play it?

Not yet. We will announce it on this website when it is ready, and say where you can get it.

I found a bug or have an idea. Where do I send it?

Use the form below. It goes straight to our team’s issue tracker; you need no account. To hear back from us, write an email instead.

What does 4 Friends collect about me?

This website uses no analytics, ads or tracking, and the feedback form sends only what you type. The privacy policy has the details, and we will extend it to cover the game before it is released.

Send Feedback

Found a bug or missing something? Tell us here; no account needed. The game will get the same form.

Kind

Your report goes to 4 Friends’ private issue tracker. Leave out anything personal you don’t want us to read; to hear back, write to us by email instead.
